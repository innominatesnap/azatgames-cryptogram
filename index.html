CryptoGram

Decode number-coded quotes and send cryptograms to friends.

Try a line

Tap a number, then a letter. A correct letter fills in everywhere that number appears. On a keyboard, use Tab, the arrow keys, and Enter.

How it works

  1. 01

    Daily quote

    A new quote each day. Letters are swapped for numbers, and each number stands for a single letter.

  2. 02

    Send to friends

    Compose a message, turn it into a cryptogram, and send it for a friend to solve.

  3. 03

    Hints and stars

    Hint points lower your stars. A clean solve, inside par, earns 3.
